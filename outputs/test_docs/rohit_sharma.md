## Notable Innings

### Triple Century in ODIs (2023)
**Match:** India vs Australia at MCG, Melbourne  
**Score:** 319* (all out)  
**Context:** Against England's 297/8, India needed 298 runs to win. Rohit scored 319* off 366 balls, including 10 sixes and 12 fours. This became the highest individual score in ODI history for India.

### 188* vs Pakistan (2017)
**Match:** India vs Pakistan at Dubai  
**Score:** 188*  
**Context:** Against Pakistan's 218/9, India needed 219 runs to win. Rohit scored 188* off 188 balls, including 9 sixes and 14 fours. This was his 50th ODI century and marked his 7th ODI triple century.

### 231* vs New Zealand (2022)
**Match:** India vs New Zealand at Eden Park, Auckland  
**Score:** 231*  
**Context:** Against New Zealand's 245/9, India needed 246 runs to win. Rohit scored 231* off 231 balls, including 8 sixes and 15 fours. This was his 7th ODI triple century.

### 166* vs West Indies (2023)
**Match:** India vs West Indies at The Oval, London  
**Score:** 166*  
**Context:** Against West Indies' 173/9, India needed 174 runs to win. Rohit scored 166* off 166 balls, including 6 sixes and 12 fours. This was his 11th ODI triple century.

## Conclusion

Rohit Sharma is one of the greatest batsmen in cricket history. His ability to score centuries in all formats, his triple-century record in ODIs, and his consistent match-winning performances make him a legend in Indian cricket.
